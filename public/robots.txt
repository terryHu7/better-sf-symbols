# One page, and it should be findable. Both titles carry the query someone
# would actually type — 「SF Symbols 图标符号」 on the Chinese side, "sf symbols
# preview" on the English one — and both `meta.description`s repeat it in their
# first few words. That is deliberate and it is written down beside the strings
# in app/messages.ts; a title rewrite that drops those words is a real cost, not
# a wording preference. This file answered 404 until it existed at all.
User-agent: *
Allow: /

# 7,988 monochrome mask PNGs. They are the product's artwork, not pictures worth
# indexing — every one of them is a white shape on transparency, meaningless in
# an image search and identical to Apple's own. Keeping crawlers out of them is
# most of this file's point: it is the difference between a crawl of one page
# and a crawl of eight thousand.
Disallow: /symbols/
